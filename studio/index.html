Counterfactual Step-Level Credit Assignment (CSAO)

Surgical Pivot-Error Credit

In multi-step reasoning, Small Language Models frequently generate 2 or 3 correct deduction steps before committing a calculation slip. Instead of penalizing the entire trajectory, CSAO locates the exact pivot step where deduction broke, rewarding preceding valid steps and penalizing only the point of failure.
